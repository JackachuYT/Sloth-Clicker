### What's in the game

**Shop**
- ⬆️ Click Power — increase your points per click
- 🎯 Critical Hit — 20% chance to deal 3× damage
- 👆 Double Tap — every click counts twice
- 🦥 Baby Sloth — auto income +1/s each
- 🦥🦥 Sloth Gang — auto income +5/s each
- 👑 Sloth Empire — auto income +20/s each
- 🥇 Golden Sloth — doubles all auto income permanently
- 🌀 Sloth Nexus — auto income +100/s each
- ⏰ Time Boost — 30s of 2× all auto income
- 🧲 Click Magnet — each click also gains 10% of your auto rate
- 💣 Score Bomb — instant point burst
- 🌈 Rainbow Event (secret) — 5s of random ×1–100 multiplier
- ⚡ 10× Multiplier (secret) — multiplies your current rate by 10

**Secret Codes** *(enter in the shop's Redeem Code box)*
- `SL0TH` — unlocks the Secret Items section
- `ZZZZZZ` — 10 free Baby Sloths fall asleep into your team
- `BIGBUX` — +50,000 pts instantly
- `LAZYBOY` — 60s of 2× auto income

**Achievements** — 27 total across Clicking, Score, Upgrades, Events, and Prestige categories. Complete them all to unlock the secret **What Now?** achievement.

**Prestige** — reset your run for a permanent multiplier boost and bragging rights.
